# Privacy Policy for mySnapgoals

Effective date: 2026-03-23

mySnapgoals respects your privacy. This app is designed to work primarily on your device and does not require creating an account or connecting to a backend service.

## Information We Collect

mySnapgoals stores the information you enter directly in the app, such as:

- goals and to-dos
- progress history related to your goals
- app settings and preferences
- Pomodoro-related preferences

This information is stored locally on your device.

## Information We Do Not Collect

mySnapgoals does not:

- create user accounts
- operate its own backend server
- collect personal information on a remote server
- sell your data
- use advertising SDKs
- use analytics or tracking SDKs

## Permissions

mySnapgoals may request the following permissions only to support app features:

- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: used to show notifications for timers and app activity when enabled by the user
- `VIBRATE`: used for timer or reminder vibration feedback
- `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`: used to keep the Pomodoro timer active and visible while running

These permissions are used only for app functionality on your device.

## Data Sharing

mySnapgoals does not share your data with third parties.

## Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored locally on your device using on-device storage mechanisms. Because no backend service is used, your information is not transmitted to our servers.

You are responsible for the security of your device and any device-level backups that may apply.

## Children

mySnapgoals is not directed specifically to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a backend service or remote account system.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future if the app's features or data practices change. Any updates should be published together with the latest app release information.
